Golf: turn strength into rotation

Friday's golf block is in your schedule above. Use this short routine before every round or range session.

10-minute pre-round warm-up

  1. Cat-cow 8 slow reps
  2. World's greatest stretch 4 each side
  3. Open-book rotation 6 each side
  4. Band pull-aparts 15 reps
  5. Lateral lunge 6 each side
  6. Hip hinge, club across shoulders 10 reps
  7. Half swings building to full 10 swings

How the gym helps your swing

Glutes are your engineHip thrusts and split squats build the hip drive that powers the downswing.

Upper back turns, lower back stays quietThoracic rotation work lets you turn fully without twisting through the lumbar spine.

A braced core transfers the powerPallof presses and carries teach you to resist rotation, so the energy goes into the club.

Timing mattersAvoid your hardest leg day the day before a competitive round. Swap Thursday and Friday if you need to.

How to progress

These rules apply to every strength day. Each exercise shows what you did last time and what to aim for today.

Use double progressionWhen you hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean form, add weight next time. The page suggests the jump for you.

Train close to failureRPE is effort out of 10. RPE 8 means you could do about 2 more reps.

Tempo, then weightTempo 3-0-1 means 3 seconds down, no pause, 1 second up. Don't trade the slow lowering for heavier weight.

Log your top setEnter the weight and reps of your best working set. That's what the reminders and chart use.

Sleep is part of the planAim for 7–9 hours. Muscle is built while you recover, not while you train.

Missed a day?Do the next session in order. Never do two glute days back to back.
